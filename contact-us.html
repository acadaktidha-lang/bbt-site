Common Questions About Working With Us

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?+

Within 24 hours on business days. For urgent projects, put the word urgent in your message and we will prioritize it. GCC clients get responses aligned to Gulf business hours.

Is the initial consultation really free?+

Yes, completely free and with no obligation. It is a 30-minute call where we understand your project and give you an honest assessment. There is no sales pitch, and if we are not the right fit we will tell you.

What information should I include in my message?+

The more context the better: your industry, current challenges, goals, timeline, and budget range. But do not overthink it. Even a brief description is enough to start the conversation.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?+

Yes. We have engagement models for businesses of every size, from single-location startups through to enterprise chains, and we will say plainly which one fits your budget.

Can we meet in person if we are in Wyoming or Dubai?+

Yes. We welcome in-person meetings at our Sheridan, Wyoming headquarters and our Dubai office. Mention it in your message and we will schedule accordingly.

What if I am not ready to start yet?+

That is completely fine. We are happy to answer questions, give advice, and stay in touch. Many clients get in contact two or three months before they are ready to begin.
