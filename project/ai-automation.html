FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does AI automation cost?
Simple workflows start at $500–2,000. Complex multi-system builds range from $5,000–20,000+. Every project starts with a free audit so you know the exact cost before committing to anything.
How long does it take to build?
Standard automations take 2–4 weeks. Complex builds take 6–10 weeks. We deliver in phases — you can start seeing results as early as week 2.
Will it work with the tools we already use?
Yes. We work with HubSpot, Salesforce, Odoo, Zoho, Slack, WhatsApp, SAP, Shopify, and many more. If your tool has an API, we can connect it. If it doesn't, we will find a workaround.
Is WhatsApp Business API available in UAE?
Yes — fully available for business messaging and automation. WhatsApp voice and video calling has VoIP restrictions in UAE, but chatbots and messaging work fully.
Do we need to replace our current software?
Almost never. We connect to your existing tools. We only recommend a platform change if your current system has no API and no workaround exists.
What if the automation breaks or stops working?
We monitor all automations after launch. You get error alerts and a dedicated support contact. We offer SLA tiers from business hours to 24/7 coverage.
Can you build chatbots that speak Arabic?
Yes — bilingual Arabic and English chatbots on WhatsApp and web. Our bots use Arabic NLP and switch language automatically based on what the customer writes.
Do you work with businesses outside the GCC?
Yes — we work with clients in the USA, UK, and other markets. We use time zone overlap and asynchronous delivery so you are never waiting for a response.
