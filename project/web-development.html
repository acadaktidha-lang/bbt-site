FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?
It depends on what you need. A simple business website usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. A corporate site is typically $3,500 to $8,000. An e-commerce store starts around $5,000 and goes up from there depending on features. We start every project with a free audit, so you get a real number before you commit to anything.
How long does it take to build a website?
A standard business website takes about 2 to 3 weeks. A corporate site or online store takes 4 to 6 weeks. Larger custom builds can take 6 to 10 weeks. We work in phases, so you'll see progress within the first week.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Yes. Every site we build is mobile-first, which means we design for phones first and scale up from there. We test on real devices, not just desktop screens, so the experience holds up wherever your visitors are.
Can you build Arabic websites?
Yes. We build full right-to-left layouts, Arabic fonts, and bilingual switching between English and Arabic. We test everything with native Arabic speakers so it reads naturally.
Do I need to find my own hosting?
No. We can set you up with GCC-optimized hosting or work with a hosting provider you already use. We'll explain the trade-offs so you can pick what fits your visitors best.
What if I need changes after launch?
Every project includes a 30-day support period. After that, you can choose a monthly care plan or pay for updates as you need them. You can reach us directly by email or WhatsApp, no ticket systems.
Will I be able to update the website myself?
Yes. We build on WordPress, Shopify, or a custom CMS with an easy-to-use admin panel. You'll get a training session and simple written guides, so you're confident making changes on your own.
Do you work with businesses outside the GCC?
Yes. We work with clients across the GCC, USA, and UK. Our team overlaps with all three time zones, so you're never waiting long for a reply.
