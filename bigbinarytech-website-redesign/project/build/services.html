Common questions about our technology services

Which service should my business start with?+

We always begin with a free discovery call. Most businesses start with whatever hurts most right now, usually AI automation or Odoo ERP, then expand as part of a connected roadmap. No pressure, no upsell, just a clear plan.

How long does a project take?+

A focused website or Odoo setup takes 4 to 8 weeks. A full custom software project with integrations runs 8 to 16 weeks. Either way, we deliver in phases so you see working features every month, not just at the very end.

Can your services integrate with our existing systems?+

Yes. We integrate with most ERP systems, accounting tools, payment gateways and CRM platforms. If your system has no ready connector, we build a custom API to bridge the gap, so you stop doing manual work.

How does AI automation reduce business costs?+

By automating repetitive tasks and connecting your tools, your team stops doing manual busywork. Most clients reduce manual operations by up to 70% with our AI automation services. That means lower labour costs, fewer errors and faster turnaround.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?+

Yes. We are based in Pakistan and actively work with clients in the GCC, the United States, the United Kingdom and across Europe. We adapt to your time zone and business culture, with support available where needed.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?+

Every software project includes post-launch support. We also offer monthly care plans for updates, security patches and continuous optimization as your business grows. You are never left alone after go-live.
