// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an Odoo project with Big Binary Tech actually involve?+

We start with discovery workshops to map how each department works today, then issue a written blueprint covering modules, integrations, custom development, phases, and a go-live date. From there we configure, migrate your data, run user acceptance testing with your team, train your users, and stay on for support once you are live.

Can you take over an Odoo system another partner set up?+

Yes. We regularly take over half finished or poorly configured Odoo instances. We audit the current setup, document what works and what needs rebuilding, clean up the data, and give you a clear plan to get the system usable again before we touch any code.

Do you build custom Odoo modules, or only configure the standard ones?+

Both, in that order. We configure standard Odoo first because it is faster to deliver and cheaper to upgrade. Where the standard modules genuinely cannot cover a workflow, we build custom modules that follow Odoo conventions, so future version upgrades do not break your system.

How do you migrate data from spreadsheets or our legacy ERP?+

We extract your existing records, clean and map them to the Odoo data model, then load them into a staging environment where your team reconciles opening balances, stock counts, and customer histories. Only once the numbers match do we migrate to production, so you never go live on data you do not trust.

How do you train our team and support us after go-live?+

Training is part of the delivery, not an extra. We run role based sessions for finance, HR, warehouse, and sales users and hand over written documentation. After go-live we stay close through your first reporting cycles and offer a support retainer covering bug fixes, enhancements, and Odoo version upgrades.

How much does Odoo implementation cost?+

It depends on scope. A standard Odoo Community setup usually starts around $3,000 to $8,000. Adding Enterprise features and custom modules typically runs $10,000 to $30,000. Larger, multi-department rollouts can go higher. The free audit exists so you get a real number for your business instead of guessing.

How long does Odoo implementation take?+

A standard implementation usually takes 4 to 6 weeks. Builds with custom modules run 8 to 12 weeks. Migration-only projects take 2 to 4 weeks. Either way, you are not waiting until the end to see something working, because core modules are usually usable within the first couple of weeks.

What is the difference between Odoo Community and Enterprise?+

Community is free and open-source. Enterprise adds features like advanced reporting, mobile apps, and official Odoo support, for a licence fee. We will tell you honestly which one fits your business during the audit. We do not push Enterprise if Community does the job.

Can Odoo handle Arabic and RTL?+

Yes, with a full Arabic interface, right-to-left layout, and bilingual switching. We test with native Arabic speakers and make sure government report formats like WPS and ZATCA come out correctly, not just translated.

Do you work with businesses outside the GCC?+

Yes. We deliver for clients across the GCC, the USA, and the UK, with an async delivery model so time zones do not slow the project down.
