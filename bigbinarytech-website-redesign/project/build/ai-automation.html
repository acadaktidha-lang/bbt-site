// CHATBOTS, RAG AND LLM SYSTEMS

Answers From Your Own Data, Not From the Open Internet

A general purpose chatbot will confidently invent an answer about your refund policy. One built on your documents will quote the policy and tell you which page it came from. That difference is the whole reason to build rather than subscribe.

What we build chatbots for

  • Customer support. Handles the questions your team answers twenty times a day, and hands over to a person the moment it is out of its depth.
  • Internal knowledge base. Staff ask in plain language instead of hunting through a shared drive for the current version of a document.
  • Website assistant. Answers pre-sales questions, qualifies the enquiry and passes a complete lead to your CRM.
  • Inside your product. An assistant embedded in your own SaaS or portal, scoped to what each user is allowed to see.

What we ground them on

  • PDFs and scanned files. Manuals, reports and anything already sitting in a folder rather than a database.
  • Company documentation. Policies, SOPs and process guides, kept in step as they are revised.
  • Contracts and legal documents. Clause lookup and comparison, with the source paragraph shown every time.
  • HR material. Leave, payroll and onboarding questions, answered without routing every one to HR.
  • Product documentation. Specs, release notes and support articles, for customers and for your own team.

Access is scoped before anything is indexed. An assistant only ever reads the documents you point it at, permissions follow the ones already set on those files, and every question and answer is logged so you can audit what it told people.

// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which processes should we automate first?+

The best first candidates are high-volume, rule-based tasks that a person repeats the same way every time, such as data entry between systems, invoice handling, approval routing, and status reporting. Our process audit ranks your workflows by hours consumed and error rate, so the first automation we build is the one that pays back fastest.

Do we need to replace our current systems?+

No. Automation sits on top of what you already run. We connect your ERP, CRM, POS, finance tools, and spreadsheets through APIs and integrations rather than forcing a migration. If a system genuinely cannot support your growth we will say so, but replacing it is a separate decision and never a precondition for automating.

How long does an automation project take?+

A focused single-workflow automation usually goes live within a few weeks of the process audit. Broader programs that span several departments and integrations run longer and are delivered in phases, so you see working automation early instead of waiting for one big launch at the end.

Is our data safe with AI assistants?+

Yes. We scope exactly which data an assistant can access, keep it inside systems you control, and apply role-based permissions so staff only see what they are allowed to see. Sensitive actions are gated behind human approval and every step is logged for audit.

What happens after the automation goes live?+

We monitor the workflows, alert on failures, and fix breaks as your systems and rules change. You also get documentation and team training so your staff can run and adjust the automation themselves, then we help you extend it to the next process when you are ready.

Which AI model do you use?+

Whichever fits the job and your constraints. OpenAI, Claude and Gemini are all strong, and they differ on cost, speed, context length and where the data is processed. If your data has to stay in a particular region, or inside your own cloud tenancy, that decides it before anything else does. We tell you what we recommend and why, and the system is built so the model can be swapped later without a rebuild.

Will our documents be used to train someone else’s model?+

No. We use business tier APIs where the provider does not train on submitted data, and your documents stay in storage you control. Where data cannot leave your environment at all, we say so early, because that rules some options out and changes what the build costs.

How do we know the answers are right?+

Every answer cites the document and section it came from, so an answer is checkable in one click rather than taken on trust. Before launch we test the assistant against a set of real questions with known correct answers, and where it should not guess, we configure it to say it does not know and route the question to a person.

Do we need a data warehouse before we can use AI?+

Not for a document based assistant, which reads files as they are. You do need one the moment you want answers about numbers, because a model cannot reconcile five systems that disagree. If that is where you are heading we usually build the warehouse first, since it pays for itself on reporting alone.

How much does AI automation cost?+

Simple workflows start at $500 to $2,000. Complex multi-system builds range from $5,000 to $20,000 and up. Every project starts with a free audit so you know the exact cost before committing to anything.

Will it work with the tools we already use?+

Yes. We work with HubSpot, Salesforce, Odoo, Zoho, Slack, WhatsApp, SAP, Shopify, and many more. If your tool has an API, we can connect it. If it does not, we will find a workaround.

Is WhatsApp Business API available in UAE?+

Yes, and it is fully available for business messaging and automation. WhatsApp voice and video calling has VoIP restrictions in the UAE, but chatbots and messaging work fully.

What if the automation breaks or stops working?+

We monitor all automations after launch. You get error alerts and a dedicated support contact. We offer SLA tiers from business hours through to 24/7 coverage.

Can you build chatbots that speak Arabic?+

Yes, bilingual Arabic and English chatbots on WhatsApp and web. Our bots use Arabic NLP and switch language automatically based on what the customer writes.

Do you work with businesses outside the GCC?+

Yes, we work with clients in the USA, UK, and other markets. We use time zone overlap and asynchronous delivery so you are never waiting for a response.
