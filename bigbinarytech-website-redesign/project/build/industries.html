// INDUSTRY FAQ

Questions about industry solutions

Do you have experience in my specific industry?+

Yes. We have delivered solutions across 15+ industries including retail, manufacturing, healthcare, education, real estate, financial services, hospitality, logistics, construction and government. Every project starts with deep industry discovery.

How do you ensure compliance with industry regulations?+

Compliance is built into the foundation of every solution. We research your industry's regulatory requirements during discovery, such as FBR, ZATCA, HIPAA or GDPR, build compliance features into the architecture and validate before go-live.

Can you integrate with the tools we already use?+

Absolutely. We integrate with industry-standard platforms including medical devices, ERP systems, accounting software, payment gateways, logistics APIs and government portals. Custom API development is available when native integration is not possible.

How long does an industry solution take to implement?+

Simple industry configurations take 4 to 6 weeks. Custom industry platforms take 8 to 16 weeks. Enterprise multi-system integrations take 4 to 6 months. We deliver in phases so you see value every month.

What if our workflows are not covered by standard software?+

That is exactly why businesses choose Big Binary Tech. We do not force your workflows into generic software. We build custom modules, workflows and interfaces that match how your industry actually operates.

What support do you offer after launch?+

A 30-day post-launch support period is included. After that, monthly industry care plans provide dedicated support, system updates, compliance updates and continuous optimization. We grow with your industry.
