// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom POS system cost?+

It depends on the size of your build. A single-terminal retail POS usually costs $6,000 to $12,000. A multi-location retail or restaurant POS runs $15,000 to $45,000. A full enterprise or franchise POS with cloud sync starts around $40,000 and can go above $120,000. We start every project with a free audit, so you know your real number before you commit to anything.

How long does it take to build a POS system?+

A single-location retail or restaurant POS takes 6 to 9 weeks. A multi-location or franchise POS takes 10 to 16 weeks. An enterprise cloud POS with full inventory sync takes 16 to 28 weeks. We can also launch in phases, so one branch goes live while we finish the rest.

Will my POS work with the hardware I already own?+

In most cases, yes. We work with standard terminals, card readers, receipt printers, and barcode scanners, including Verifone, PAX, and Ingenico. We check your existing hardware during the discovery call and tell you early if anything needs replacing.

Can you build a POS with Arabic support?+

Yes. We build full right-to-left checkout screens, Arabic receipts, bilingual staff interfaces, and ZATCA-compliant Arabic e-invoicing. We test everything with native Arabic speakers so it reads naturally.

Does the POS work if the internet goes down?+

Yes. Offline mode comes as standard on our retail and restaurant builds. Transactions save locally and sync automatically once the connection comes back, so your till never stops.

Where will my sales and inventory data be hosted?+

You choose. We offer GCC local hosting on AWS Dubai or Azure UAE, EU hosting for GDPR compliance, or US hosting. We will walk you through the pros and cons for your market during the discovery call.

Will I own the source code and data?+

Yes. Full source code and data ownership transfers to you once the project is paid in full. No licensing fees, no vendor lock-in. You are free to take the system to any developer in future.

Do you work with businesses outside the Gulf?+

Yes. We work with clients across the US, UK, and other markets. Our team overlaps your working hours and delivers async, so you are never stuck waiting on a reply.
