// OUR STORY

The smart way forward, since day one

Big Binary Tech started in September 2015 with one simple goal: bring powerful enterprise technology to businesses of every size, not just big corporations. What began as a small software team has grown into a full digital transformation partner, offering Odoo ERP implementation, AI automation, custom software development and POS solutions to clients across the world.

For us, "Binary" means a choice: between doing things the old way, or doing them the smart way. Every business we work with is choosing to move forward. Our job is to make that move easy, reliable and worth it.

We are Pakistan based and PSEB registered, and we hold ourselves to the standard our international clients expect. Global delivery, local accountability.

// ABOUT BBT

Questions about working with us

Is Big Binary Tech a registered and compliant software company in Pakistan?+

Yes. Big Binary Tech is registered with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) as a taxpayer, registered with the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB) as an IT & ITeS exporter, and holds membership with the Lahore Chamber of Commerce & Industry (LCCI). These registrations mean every engagement is invoiced, taxed, and documented the way an audit-ready enterprise vendor should be, not an informal freelance setup.

What is Big Binary Tech's partnership with NAVTTC?+

Big Binary Tech holds a public-private partnership with the National Vocational & Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC), Pakistan's federal body for technical and vocational training. This partnership supports workforce development and technical training initiatives, and reflects our standing as a recognized contributor to Pakistan's IT talent pipeline, not just a service vendor.

Why do your registrations and certifications matter to clients outside Pakistan?+

For GCC, USA, and UK clients, our PSEB registration, FBR tax compliance, LCCI membership, and Cisco partnership give you the same vendor due-diligence trail you'd expect from a locally registered agency, at Pakistan-based delivery costs. It also means contracts, invoicing, and any compliance documentation you need for procurement are already in place, not something we scramble to produce mid-project.
