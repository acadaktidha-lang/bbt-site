// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom software project take?+

It depends on scope. A focused internal tool or portal is usually delivered in a matter of weeks, while a larger multi module platform runs over several months. We break every project into short sprints with a working demo at the end of each one, so you see progress early and the timeline stays predictable.

How much does custom software development cost?+

Cost is driven by scope, integrations, and how much design work is involved. After a free discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed scope, milestones, and pricing so you know what you are committing to before development begins. You can also start with a smaller first phase and expand from there.

Who owns the code and the intellectual property?+

You do. Source code, documentation, designs, and infrastructure configuration are handed over to you and remain your property. There is no proprietary lock in, and you are free to host, extend, or move the application whenever you choose.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?+

Yes. Integration is one of our core strengths. We build custom APIs and connectors that link your application to Odoo and other ERP systems, CRM platforms, accounting tools, payment gateways, and third party services, so data flows between them without manual re entry.

What happens after launch?+

We stay on. Our support covers monitoring, bug fixes, security updates, and performance tuning, plus a roadmap of improvements as your business evolves. Your team also gets training and documentation so they are confident running the product day to day.
